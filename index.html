How I work

Whether I'm designing an app or untangling a business process, I follow the same three steps.

  1. 1 Listen first I start by understanding what people actually need, through questions, interviews, and a lot of listening.
  2. 2 Figure out how it works I map out the problem, challenge assumptions, and look at it from more than one perspective.
  3. 3 Make it work better I sketch, build, test, and refine, and I'm happy to let go of an idea if it isn't right for the people using it.
